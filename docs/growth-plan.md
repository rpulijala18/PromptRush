# PromptRush — NxtWave 7-Day Growth Strategy
## Campaign: "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"
**Target:** 500 Unique Student Registrations in 7 Days  
**Budget:** ₹2,000 Total Promotional Spend  
**Primary Cohort:** Final-Year Engineering Students (Graduating Class of 2027)  

---

### Executive Summary

Most educational workshops struggle with passive landing pages that demand high commitment upfront from skeptical students. **PromptRush** flips the funnel through a 60-second micro-challenge: students test their AI prompting instincts, receive an educational score, uncover prompt improvements for campus placement interviews, and naturally transition into the free workshop to build their first complete AI project.

```
DISCOVER (WhatsApp/Clubs) ➔ PLAY (60s Challenge) ➔ LEARN (PromptScore) ➔ REGISTER (Free Workshop) ➔ SHARE (Peer Viral Loop)
```

---

### 1. Target Audience Understanding (Class of 2027)

#### Profile & Placement Mindset
- **Who:** 7th & 8th semester engineering students across Computer Science, IT, ECE, and allied engineering branches.
- **Urgent Problem:** Placement drives demand practical, demonstrable AI experience rather than superficial buzzwords. Students feel behind on AI tools and dread technical interviews.
- **Core Motivations:**
  - **Resume Differentiator:** Need verifiable AI projects on their GitHub/portfolio before upcoming on-campus drives.
  - **Overcoming AI Imposter Syndrome:** Want practical hands-on building without complex theoretical math.
  - **Immediate Peer Comparison:** Motivated by competitive challenge formats and batchmate leaderboards.

#### Why Students Register for the Workshop
1. **Zero Barrier Entry:** Free 60-minute duration is low-friction and fits tight semester schedules.
2. **Definite Milestone:** "Build Your First AI Project" guarantees an artifact to showcase.
3. **Bridge from PromptRush:** Having completed a 60-second challenge, students are primed to explore full project development.

### 3. Seven-Day Execution Timeline

- **Day 1 — Launch & Funnel Instrument Testing (Pace: 25)**
  - Deploy PromptRush with UTM tracking.
  - Test the entire student loop (Challenge ➔ Score ➔ Register ➔ WhatsApp Share).
  - Verify analytics ingestion and A/B variant assignment.
- **Day 2 — Campus Ambassador Onboarding (Pace: 45)**
  - Onboard 10 student campus representatives. Provide customized referral links (`/challenge?ref=AMB_COLLEGE`).
  - Distribute quick-start kits with WhatsApp message templates and scoreboard previews.
- **Day 3 — Technical Club Distribution & WhatsApp Push (Pace: 60)**
  - Launch joint announcements across 5 college coding club Discord/WhatsApp groups.
  - First placement group announcement drop by class representatives.
- **Day 4 — Funnel & Drop-off Diagnostic (Pace: 75)**
  - Review Command Center metrics: Analyze visit-to-start, completion, and registration conversion.
  - Review A/B testing lab results (Variant A vs Variant B).
- **Day 5 — Conversion Rate Optimization & Retargeting (Pace: 85)**
  - Implement winning CTA variant across hero banners.
  - Re-engage scored challenge participants who completed the trial but have not yet registered.
- **Day 6 — Viral Peer Challenge Surge (Pace: 100)**
  - Introduce college batch competition: announce leading college leaderboards on WhatsApp.
  - Ambassador final sprint with performance milestone bonuses.
- **Day 7 — Final Push & Registration Consolidation (Pace: 110)**
  - "Last 24 Hours to Register" urgency push across community channels.
  - Export registered cohort data; reconcile attribution channels; prepare post-campaign growth report.

### 5. Assumptions, Key Risks & Mitigation

- **Risk: Ambassador Inactivity.** If 3 ambassadors underperform, yield drops by 60 registrations.  
  *Mitigation:* Daily leaderboard tracking on Growth Command Center; swap inactive ambassadors on Day 3 with high-yield applicants.
- **Risk: Challenge Drop-off.** Students might abandon Part B if typing on mobile is tedious.  
  *Mitigation:* Prompt booster chips included in UI so students can build strong prompts with 1-click taps on 360px screens.
- **Risk: Referral Saturation.** WhatsApp shares could feel spammy if overused.  
  *Mitigation:* Honest, score-based challenge format focused on placement preparation rather than misleading monetary incentives.
