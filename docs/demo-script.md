### [0:00 – 0:25] The Problem & Growth Challenge
*(Screen: Camera on candidate / Switch to PromptRush homepage)*

> "Hi everyone! The objective for this challenge was clear: achieve **500 student registrations within 7 days** for NxtWave's free online workshop, *Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes*, with a tight promotional budget of just **₹2,000**, targeting final-year engineering students graduating in 2027.
>
> In 2027 engineering batches, students are facing upcoming campus placement drives. They are bombarded with generic webinar links every day. Traditional landing pages yield high bounce rates and expensive cost-per-click. 
> 
> To solve this, I designed and built **PromptRush**—a gamified 60-second AI prompting challenge that hooks students, proves their AI skills, delivers instant placement value, and bridges seamlessly into workshop registrations."

*(Screen: Candidate navigates to `/challenge` and clicks "Start 60s Challenge")*

>
> In **Part B**, the student becomes the builder: *Write an AI prompt that helps a final-year engineer prepare for a technical interview.*

### [1:10 – 1:40] Educational Results & Registration Flow
*(Screen: Results page `/results/[attemptId]` loads with animated score gauge & confetti)*

> 
> The student sees exact strengths, improvements, and an optimal benchmark prompt. Right below, the primary CTA connects their score to action: *'You improved in 60 seconds—now build your first project in 60 minutes.'*
